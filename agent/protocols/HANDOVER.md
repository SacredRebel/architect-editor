# HANDOVER — before a long pause, a model change or a move to another interface

Rewrite `agent\HANDOVER.md` (the letter to the next instance; one read must be enough):
1. Who I am and what I own: the repository, the branch, the paths I may write (and the one file in the map's repository).
2. How my part enters the one program (PLAN §0): Ctrl+R, the records, the round trip, the port notes.
3. What I built, in order, with commits (git log --oneline is the source).
4. What works and what is PROVEN: the check, picture or number that proves each, with its path.
5. What is not done; what I was in the middle of (file, function, line).
6. The next three steps.
7. What I need from Johny, the architect and the other lanes.
8. The pitfalls I hit (and their guards).
9. Every command I run (build, check, export, test, the map's checks), with its exact line.
10. Where the reports, NEW.md, the port notes, the evidence live.
Then the normal session end (protocols\SESSION.md), and a commit "A: transfer — home and HANDOVER" (or "A: handover").
Nothing in the conversation is a handover; only the file is.
